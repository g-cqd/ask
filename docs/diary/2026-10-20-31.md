### Mistakes

- **My first resume rule broke every existing session.** I refused an unbound session unless `--provider` was passed, which is
  right for a named connection and wrong for the unnamed provider that history had always used. Found by rereading my own
  design, not by a test; reproduced on the real binary before fixing.
- **Mutation round one was partly wrong.** One mutation did not compile (an unused variable, and warnings are errors), one
  "survived" only because my test filter left out the suite that holds its test, and one target had been reformatted by
  swift-format and never applied. All three were redone: 12 of 12 killed.
- **The driver's own logs vanished from `/tmp`** mid-run and crashed it, while other files there survived. Cause unknown, perhaps
  another agent's cleanup of `ask-conn*`. I checked the source for mutation markers by grep (none), then moved the driver's
  output to `~/Library/Caches`. My first grep for the originals used `+` as a regex operator and looked like residue; it was not.
- Analyzer directives had to be placed three times before they sat on the line the tool reports.

### Not verified, and a follow-up
