Not modelled: the session file's lock and rewrite (ADR-0025), read from `SessionFile`, `SessionLock` and `SessionFileIO`. Two candidates from
reading, neither probed: `SessionFile.begin` does not close the descriptor `writeExclusively` returned when `SessionLock.adopt` throws

runs out at 1,000.
