width table and the realignment after contested graphemes come with the pin.

`PipelineProbe` at 120×40 and 300×80, median, layout and flush (`ScreenComposer` included), before this pin
(`f067f50`) and after:

| | 120×40 | 300×80 |

| Streamed line, layout + flush | 154 → 107 µs | 213 → 140 µs |
| Streamed line, scroll region | 162 → 113 µs | 243 → 172 µs |

10 ns a wide character. Bytes per streamed line are unchanged (101 and 102).

TerminalKit with eight more and an oracle (its ADR-0025).
