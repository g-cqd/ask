### What went wrong

- **I took ADR and diary numbers another session had already used** and found out when I wrote the files: ADR-0045 and diary 41 were
  theirs. I moved to 0046 and 42 before committing.
- **The prompt count looked like an answer and is not one.** Median 49 rows for Claude Code is not what a person types. I kept it in
  the note with that said, and did not use it to move the default.
- **The probe reads private session logs.** It stores and prints counts only, and the note says so; the numbers describe one person
  on one machine.

### Not verified

That a 20-line threshold feels right to the owner (the data says where blocks end, not where patience does), and the wheel step on a
real trackpad. Prompts: no clean measure of what a person types.
