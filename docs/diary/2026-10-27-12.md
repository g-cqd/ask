# Diary — 2026-10-27 (12)

## Job strip progress

Task worktree `ask-job-progress`, based on 0e11cbc. Added monotonic launch timestamps
from Job through JobSummary into RunnerInfo. The controller keeps one BlockProgress
per active job, preserves its start/frame across snapshots and focus changes, and
removes it when the job becomes terminal or leaves the list. The existing 200 ms
progress timer now stays wanted for job-only activity, not just model turns.

The strip reserves a blank row after status. Its geometry excludes that row from
click targets. Strip capacity accounts for input rules, the gap, and a transcript row;
small screens suppress the strip rather than allocating negative transcript height.
Trailing duration/spinner metadata keeps a four-cell gap from the clipped command.
The sidebar shares this layout, budgeting its leading divider margin so its spinner
is not clipped. Existing localized-duration and spinner settings are reused.
