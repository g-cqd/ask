## Mistakes and what they changed

- **Two mutation runs stalled.**
  - The retry tests waited on a virtual clock that a mutant never let sleep. Swift Testing's minute-grained
    time limit did not save them.
  - Aborting the command killed the mutation script before its restore trap ran, leaving a mutant in the tree.
    Caught by reading the file before committing, and restored.
  - Fixes: the retry tests use an immediate clock that records waits, so they cannot hang; the mutation script
    restores on exit and times out at 240 s, inside the command's limit.
- **The structure gate's 250-line ceiling** caught my loop tests at 265 lines. The scripted provider moved to
  its own file, amended into the commit that crossed the line.
- **The new dead-code analyzer** (another session's gate) caught two of mine: a wrapper my caching rewrite left
  unused, and a `RetryPolicy.never` nobody used. Removed and squashed into their commits.
- **Rebase conflict:** the other session had rewritten the encoder with scoped JSON helpers. Caching was
  re-applied in that style, then re-probed live.
