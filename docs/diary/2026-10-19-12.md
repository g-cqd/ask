# Diary — 2026-10-19 (12)

## TerminalKit's session, adopted (roadmap 0023)

TerminalKit `adfd404` carries 0006, 0010 and its 0015. ask pins it in two commits:
- the pin alone, which passed the release tests unchanged;
- the adoption.

**What changed:**
- `StartupSequence` and `TerminalTeardown` are gone.
- The screen runs inside `TerminalSession(modes: .fullScreen, keyboardProtocol: .probe)`.
- `SignalShutdown` leaves the session. The screen's own `leave()` then does nothing, so the terminal
  sees one exit.
- Input passes through `session.observe(_:)`. It consumes the terminal's answer to the keyboard query and
  pushes disambiguation when kitty or ghostty answer. Wezterm with its default config and Terminal.app
  answer device attributes only, so they keep the legacy keys. Nothing waits for the answer
  (TerminalKit ADR-0020).

## Idle memory: not attributable

Interleaved runs, six each, RSS and physical footprint (`footprint`):

| Build | RSS median | Footprint median |
|---|---|---|
| base | 8,448 KiB | 2,976 KiB |
| pin only | 8,496 KiB | 3,008 KiB |

The live heap is the same: `heap` reports 560,336 B against 560,576 B, with 7 more nodes. The difference is
dirty "Malloc Small" pages, about six of 16 KiB, and 32 KiB more clean `__TEXT`. That is the size of the
page-layout noise diary 04 could not attribute. A second session of three runs read 80 KiB of RSS and
48 KiB of footprint. Not claimed as free, and not trimmed. The base was already over the 8 MiB RSS budget;
compact cells (TerminalKit 0007) remain the lever.
