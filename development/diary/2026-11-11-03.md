## What the probe taught me

- The first run found no question at all. The probes' terminal starts ask with `--yes`, under which a command runs unasked: the
  command had run, and the probe was waiting for a question that must not come. The probe now asks for approvals, and checks the
  `--yes` case on its own.
- The command received `/var/…`, where the probe wrote `/private/var/…`: a link's address is the standard form of the path, the
  same file. The probe compares the files.
- A second click in the same cell within the double-click time selects the word and opens nothing. The probe waits between clicks.
  A person who clicks a link twice quickly gets a selection, as before openers.
