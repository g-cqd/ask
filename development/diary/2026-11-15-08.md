Roadmap 0234 step 2: the protocol's library met the conversation. `ask serve` now makes a conversation for each `session/new`,
in the folder the client names, and a client can prompt it. Still hidden from the help: no tool calls are shown, nothing can be
approved, nothing can be cancelled.

the same `conversationLoop`), `ask serve` handing them to `AskACP` as its session source, and the probe, which the nightly sweep
takes with the others.
