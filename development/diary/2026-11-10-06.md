# 2026-11-10 (06): reply-path existence belongs to the read policy (n33, t214, roadmap 0215)

## Claim: lexical workspace containment is enough for a relative reply-path stat

**REFUTED.** The old TUI lookup used FileManager.fileExists with no policy and followed symlinks under the lexical workspace.
`ReplyPathExistenceTests` pins that behaviour before the correction: protected files, a symlinked directory/leaf and an outside
candidate are reported as existing by the old lookup. Two tests fail with five issues on the deliberately old lookup body.

The replacement moves the lookup to AskTools and passes the current policy from AgentConversation, intersected with session
narrowing. The TUI has no filesystem lookup of its own; without a host-supplied policy lookup, relative references remain text.
ScreenSession wires the conversation's lookup into the renderer before opening updates are applied.

- Canonicalise only the caller-supplied workspace; the model-supplied candidate must be absolute, lexically beneath it, contain
  no NUL or empty/dot components, and pass read policy for the root, candidate and traversed directories.
- Open each candidate directory relative to the preceding descriptor with O_NOFOLLOW; leaf metadata uses AT_SYMLINK_NOFOLLOW.
  A symlink is not followed, even when its target is readable. No candidate realpath or existence stat precedes policy checking.
- Bounded cache (4,096 answers) is per conversation, not process-global. A changed workspace or effective policy clears all
  answers before reuse, including negative answers. Files created/deleted under an unchanged policy can still need a fresh
  conversation or cache turnover; this change does not claim live filesystem invalidation.
- Symlinked relative references now stay text; absolute links still require a person's click to open. This entry does not
  claim a hostile process cannot rename workspace directories concurrently, or that unrelated path-resolution code was audited.

## Cost

`Scripts/measure-reply-paths.py`: actual production checker and sandbox sources compiled together with swiftc -O, removing only
AskAgentCore's module import to compile that subset. Nine interleaved samples of 1,000 checks, disposable one-file workspace:

| Check | Median us / full range |
|---|---|
| FileManager.fileExists, uncached old primitive | 1.508 / 1.485–1.590 |
| New checker, fresh cache each call | 24.576 / 24.422–25.061 |
| New checker, repeated path in one cache | 0.264 / 0.253–0.271 |

The former TUI also cached results; the primitive comparison is not a claim of end-to-end speedup. This measures the added
cold-check safety cost and bounded warm path, not a frame or startup budget. Machine shared, no local mutation testing.
