# 2026-11-08 (01): the diary resumes; what the probes and the mutation gate found since 4 November

The owner chose on 2026-11-08 to keep the diary (rationalisation note, §5.3). Between 5 and 7 November, 22 commits touched
`Sources`, and their probe history is in the commit messages. This entry records what was measured and found in the work since
then that is not obvious from the code.

## Claim 1: the PTY probes found three defects: two in ask, one in the probes' emulator

- **The underline in SGR's colon form.** The command-link underline is kitty's `ESC[4:1m`. pyte takes the colon for text, so it
  drew "1m" on the screen. This one was a defect in the probes' emulator, not in ask: the probes' pyte shim now reads `4:0` as 24
  and `4:N` as 4 (a2a1b71c).
- **A tab row clipped silently.** With sixteen questions at 60 columns, the question tabs past the edge were cut off with no sign.
  `probe-question-series.py` failed on the build before 9edad839. The row now shows initials, and `‹`/`›` where it is cut.
- **Back-tab did nothing.** Outside the kitty keyboard protocol, Shift+Tab arrives as `ESC [ Z`, which TerminalKit did not decode,
  so it did nothing. Found by the same probe and fixed in TerminalKit a16935d.

The full sweep is 91 of 91 at 9edad839. Not checked in a real kitty or Ghostty: the hand pointer, the dim levels, the link
underline, narrow question tabs, and back-tab in a real terminal that is not kitty.

## Claim 2: the scheduled mutation run had four consecutive causes of failure

From 3 to 8 November no scheduled Mutation run was green. Each fix exposed the next cause:

1. The tools version (fbc406e8).
2. The build timeout. A cold build of one test target on a hosted runner took over 300 s; the timeout is now 1200 s (24754a72).
3. The source standards failing on the tool's own rewrite. swift-mutation-testing v1.6.4 rewrites the changed sources in place
   with schemata. These pushed `ConfigurationFile+UI.swift` to 259 lines and `UISection+Keys.swift` to 276, and declared
   `__SwiftMutationTesting_b09f7c58`. AskCoreTests failed the unmutated baseline, and the tool stopped: "2 mutants were killed,
   but no mutant's code was ever seen running" (427a7d05).
4. Two real survivors in `UISection+Keys.swift`, now covered (427a7d05).

Measured locally, on the eleven AskAgentCore mutants from a2a1b71c, with a load average of 10:

| Timeout per mutant | Killed | Survived | Timed out | Run |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 s (the tool's default) | 0 | 0 | 11 | 20 min 22 s |
| 120 s | 9 | 2 | 0 | 6 min 1 s |

AskAgentCoreTests alone takes about 30 s here, so the default timed out every mutant and reported them all as detected. A score
of 100% made of timeouts is not evidence. `mutate-changed.sh` now passes `--timeout 120`.
