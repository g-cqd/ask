order, and returns the work: `initialize` is answered there; a prompt takes its place among its session's prompts there; the
rest (making a session, loading one, the turn) is the work, in a task of its own.

## Cancellation

Read first (Prompt Turn, "Cancellation"): `session/cancel` is a notification; the agent stops the model and the tools, sends
what it still has, and answers the prompt `cancelled`, never an error; the client answers its pending permissions as cancelled.

Prompts and cancellations are numbered as they are read. A cancellation cancels every prompt admitted so far: the turn that
runs is stopped (the task `Conversation.start` returned is cancelled, which is what Esc does), a prompt whose work had not
begun starts nothing, and a permission still asked is no longer waited for. A prompt sent after it is the next turn.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A cancelled turn answers `cancelled`, after what it still said | a directed conversation: text, cancel, more text, the end | **Holds**. The conversation says a cancelled run as a failure with no reason (`returnQueuedPrompts`, no notice); read alone that was "The turn failed." |
| A cancel right behind its prompt is not lost | both in one write; and, where the order is certain, `admit`, `cancel`, then the work | **Holds**: the conversation is never started |
| Prompts sent before the cancel go with it; one sent after is the next turn | two prompts then a cancel; a cancel then a prompt while the turn stops | **Holds** |
| A permission nobody answers does not hold a cancelled turn | an approval asked, no answer, cancel | **Holds**: the approval is denied and the turn ends |
| The tests can fail | three mutations (the early refusal, the split of prompts by the cancellation, the permission not abandoned) | **Holds**: three tests red. The first run of them never ended: a test awaited a prompt through a continuation, which a time limit cannot end. It now waits through a mailbox |
| The whole works over pipes | `probe-serve.py`, part E: a `bash` call of `sleep 47.3`, cancelled once it runs | **Holds**, four runs of about a second: `cancelled`, no `sleep` left, the next prompt answered |

Not handled: a client that cancels and never answers its permission is covered; one that never sends `session/cancel` and
never answers still holds its turn until its input ends.
