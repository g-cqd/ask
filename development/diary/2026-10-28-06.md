# Diary — 2026-10-28 (06)

## Image reading, continued (roadmap 0084)

Owner request: measure Core Image and Metal; assess BMP, AVIF, GIF, MP4 and PDF; allow video frame selection and several
images per call. Machine: Apple M3, macOS 27.0.1, Metal 4. Bench sources were in `/tmp/bench` (not kept in the repo).

### Core Image and Metal against ImageIO (scale to 2000 px, 3 runs each; lighter load than diary 05, so absolute times are lower)

| Source | ImageIO thumbnail | Core Image (`CILanczosScaleTransform`) | Metal (`MPSImageLanczosScale`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 MP JPEG | 49–82 ms, 44 MiB | 69–117 ms, 240 MiB | 81–140 ms, 169 MiB |
| 12 MP PNG | 148 ms, 86–96 MiB | 170 ms, 274 MiB | 165 ms, 181 MiB |
| 12 MP HEIC | 125–150 ms, 47 MiB | 133–155 ms, 235 MiB | 162–179 ms, 221 MiB |
| 48 MP JPEG | 83–90 ms, 56 MiB | 150–243 ms, 862 MiB | 188–199 ms, 390 MiB |
| 48 MP PNG | 485–500 ms, 158 MiB | 523–580 ms, 1024 MiB | 505–584 ms, 438 MiB |
| 48 MP HEIC | 150–187 ms, 63 MiB | 196–270 ms, 868 MiB | 296–320 ms, 408 MiB |

Peak is the process's `peak memory footprint`. **Verdict: ImageIO's thumbnail path is as fast or faster in every row and uses
3–15× less memory**; neither GPU path decodes at reduced size, and both pay a GPU texture on top of the decoded image. Kept
ImageIO; Core Image and Metal rejected for this job. (They would only pay off for filters ask does not apply.) Vision and
vImage were not preferable either (vImage: diary 05).

### Format support (ImageIO on this machine, `CGImageSourceCopyTypeIdentifiers`)

| Format | Decodes | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| BMP | yes | 36 MB for a 12 MP picture; 2000 px thumbnail in 49 ms |
| AVIF | yes (also encodes) | 129 ms to a 2000 px thumbnail |
| GIF | yes | first frame only; 148 ms |
| HEIC, TIFF, WebP | yes | |
| MP4 / MOV / M4V | AVFoundation, not ImageIO | `AVAssetImageGenerator` |
| PDF | Core Graphics (`CGPDFDocument`) | rendered by us, not decoded |

BMP, TIFF, HEIC and AVIF are now sniffed by their first bytes and sent as PNG or JPEG. Neither Anthropic nor OpenAI accepts them
as they are (not verified here against the providers' current docs).

### Video and PDF

- `AVAssetImageGenerator`, 8 frames from a 30 s H.264 file, scaled to 2000 px by the generator: 1080p, exact frames 256 ms, nearest
  keyframe 32 ms; 4K (keyframe every 250 frames), exact 1148 ms, nearest keyframe 69 ms. So sampled frames use the nearest
  keyframe; explicit times (`at`) are exact.
- PDF page to 2000 px with `CGPDFDocument`: 1–3 ms for a trivial page. **Not representative of a real page**; no real document
  was measured.

### What `read` now does

- Arguments added: `pages` ("1-3,7"), `at` (seconds, exact), `count` (spread evenly over `start`..`end`), `every` (seconds),
  `start`, `end`, `max_images` (default 8, at most 20). One call can return several images; the result line says which pages or
  times were shown, and how many more were asked for beyond the limit.
- Images and PDFs may be up to 128 MiB (text stays 16 MiB). The kind is read from the first 16 bytes before the file is read.
- Pages and frames go through the same fit as any image (2000 px, 4.5 MiB as base64, PNG/JPEG order, shrink by a quarter).
- Tests: `MediaReadTests` (formats, PDF pages and limits, video frames from a generated MP4, time planning, sniffing).

### Not done or not verified

- A video is opened by path, not by the descriptor the sandbox checked, so it has the narrower guarantee of `authorized(_:)` and
  not ADR-0085's open-then-verify. Worth a decision before relying on it under a restrictive sandbox.
- Sending a PDF as a document (Anthropic `document` blocks, OpenAI file input, Gemini inline) is not built. Rendering pages is
  the portable route. Text extraction with PDFKit would cost fewer tokens for text PDFs and was not evaluated.
- Audio, WebM/MKV and animated-GIF frame choice are not handled. Provider limits on images per request (Anthropic, OpenAI,
  Gemini) were not verified, so `max_images` caps at 20 on a guess.
- A per-request image budget (slice 6), other providers' image encoders (slice 3), the modality field (slice 4) and persistence
  by hash (slice 5) are still open. Only Anthropic receives images.
- `quality.sh --check` is clean and the whole suite passes.
