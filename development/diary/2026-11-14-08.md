# 2026-11-14 (08): serve is ACP, and the JSON-RPC was already in the family

The owner's two choices for roadmap 0234 step 2: ACP v1 is serve's protocol, and a served session is the whole conversation. With
them, an instruction: use the family's code wherever it exists, and probe for it before writing anything.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| ACP has the shape serve needs | the specification's pages read (overview, prompt turn, tool calls, session setup, elicitation, extensibility) | **Holds**: prompts, updates, permissions, cancel, load; and words I did not expect it to have (notices, compactions, usage, elicitation) |
| The family has no JSON-RPC, so serve needs one | `grep -ril jsonrpc` over the 34 repositories | **Fails**: AemiJSON ships `AemiJSONRPC` (connection, messages, line and Content-Length framing, a descriptor reader), in the revision ask already pins; swift-search serves MCP and speaks LSP through it |
| ask uses it | `grep AemiJSONRPC` in ask | **Fails**: AskMCP has its own wire (`MCPWire`, `LineSplitter`). Not touched; recorded for the audit (t271) |
| A served conversation needs its updates moved below AskTUI first (what I told the owner) | `import AskTUI` in AskSession: 61 files | **Fails**: AskSession already sees those types; the front can consume them where they are. Said in the ADR |
| A conversation can have a folder that is not the process's | `grep currentDirectoryPath` in AskSession | **Fails today**: eleven reads of the process's folder. The first refactor of the build |
