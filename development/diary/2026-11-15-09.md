The owner's report: after going from a Gemini model to another, `prompt is too long: 3330961 tokens > 1000000 maximum`, then
"does not fit the model's context window, even after compacting". The session:
`20261115T124331Z-bcda2d57.jsonl`, 3,045 records, 15.8 MB. Decision in ADR-0155.

## Red, then green

`ContextClearingTests` (one new red, three that pin what is not a clearing and were green before and after: another destination,
a rewritten request, a small fall) and `AgentLoopOversizedTests` (four, against a server double with a window of 10,000 tokens at
four bytes a token and a session of 120 KB): all four ended with the owner's error before the change.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A refused conversation is summarised in parts | 40 calls, window named by the refusal | **Holds**: one refusal, several summary requests, each call in exactly one, each part opening with the summary before it |
| With the window known nothing oversized is sent | the same, `contextWindow` set | **Holds**: no refusal |
| A refused part is halved | no window known or named | **Holds**: four refusals (the request, the whole summary, 117 KB, 58 KB), then parts that fit |
| A turn that cannot fit does not end the run | a call with 80 KB of input | **Holds**: recorded by its call, under 8,000 bytes, the parts after it summarised |

One thing the full run overturned: the check before sending was first skipped only right after a compaction made for a refusal.
Two older tests went red (`a response stopped at the full context window is compacted and continued`, and the `length` one): a
window learned from a reply that filled it, then a compaction, then the check refusing the compacted request and compacting twice
more. The check now waits for a response since the last compaction, whatever caused it; both are green again.
