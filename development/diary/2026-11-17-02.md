## Found on the way

- `ui.historyLimit` (note n29) already caps the blocks the screen keeps; its probe says it "was measured to save nothing there (the
  session holds every entry)". With the tree no longer holding them it may now save something; not measured.
- A generic `Collection.firstRange(of:)` over 4 KB lines cost 10 s of an open (37,000 lines); `memmem` costs nothing. The mutation
  that a test could not see: the open was correct either way.
- `SessionEntry.payload` was a stored `let` read in 44 places. It is a read now. The places that scan every entry for a kind
  (`nearest`, `context`, the compaction checks) read `entry.kind`, which is always held, so a scan does not read the file.
