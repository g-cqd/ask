# Diary — 2026-10-26 (01)

## Tests for two behaviours that lived in `Sources/ask` (roadmap 0028 item 2)

The audit's named pair: what `ask -p` prints, and the auth a provider resolves per request. Neither could be
tested where they were, and one of them had drifted into a near-copy of a seam that already existed.

## What was done

- `PrintRenderer` and the run's notices (`compactionNotice`, `retryNotice`) moved from `Sources/ask` into
  AskAgentCore (`Loop/PrintRenderer.swift`, `Loop/RunNotices.swift`), with the output handles injectable. The
  interactive screen and `ask -p` now share the same words through `RunNotices`, where before each had its own
  copy of the functions. 12 tests.
- `ClaudeProvider` had hand-rolled the task-cancellation and forwarding machinery that `ModelStream.after`
  already provides (and `CodexProvider` already used) — the same twenty lines, written differently. It now
  binds through the tested seam and is five lines of binding.
- The structure baseline shrank: `Sources/ask/PrintRun.swift` left it, since its renderer moved out.

## What went wrong on the way

- My own helper bit me twice in the tests: `Mutex` is noncopyable, so a test double cannot copy it out of
  `self` (a small class box fixed it), and a closure capturing `self` needed the capture spelled out. Both
  are Swift 6 strictness doing its job.
- **The stale-binary mistake again, three times in this slice alone**: red tests "failing" with expectations
  I had already rewritten, and a fix that "did not work" until I rebuilt. The failure output naming old code
  is what caught it each time. This is the third slice in a row recording it; the rule that would have
  prevented all of them is: after editing a test, always `swift build --build-tests` before believing a run.
- Two wrong expectations of mine, found by the tests and fixed against reality: the trailing empty line my
  line-splitter kept, and `usageSummary` counting cache reads and cache writes in the prompt (as Anthropic
  bills them — the probe output said "in 1 (0% cached)" and I still wrote the expectation wrong).
