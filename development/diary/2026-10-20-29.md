# Diary — 2026-10-20 (29)

## The idle-memory budget, 8 to 24 MiB

The owner asked to merge the whole work into `main` and raise the idle-memory budget to 24 MiB. All earlier work was already
on `main` (`9117f0d`); the only unmerged branch, `multiline-input`, is another agent's and was left alone. Decision: ADR-0038.

A credential-transactions probe (roadmap 0045) was started and aborted: one command chained a full build, a five-round concurrency
test and a 60 s subprocess, and showed nothing for minutes. It produced no result and is **not evidence**. 0045 stays
`not-started`. Its worktree held only the temporary probe file and was removed.
