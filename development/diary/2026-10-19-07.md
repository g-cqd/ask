# Diary — 2026-10-19 (07)

## What happened

Asked to replace Periphery with the owner's own analyzers (deadwood, dolly, arcleak, on analyzerkit) and to apply
a new rule from now on: when a refactor or factorisation is on the table, investigate what Swift itself offers
(builders, scoped closures, protocol defaults, typed throws, macros) before hand-rolling it. The rule is now white
paper section 8, and the practice is in the `ask-structure` skill and in `~/.agents/skills/structure-gates`.

Built the three from the owner's checkouts, then switched to their pinned releases once the two were shown to
agree. ADR-0022 decides the gate; ADR-0023 records the tool-contract refactor the findings led to.
