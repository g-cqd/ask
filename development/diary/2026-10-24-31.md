## Two connections of one account say so (roadmap 0046)

Worktree `ask-shared`. No Agora. The owner's real setup showed it: `work` (Codex) and the default Codex sign-in are the same account, and
`claude-a` and the default Claude sign-in are one account. Nothing said so. Two grants of one account share its usage limits (ADR-0035),
so a person who thinks they have two accounts is misled.

## What it does

- After `ask login` (default or named, Codex or Claude) a one-line notice on stderr names the other holders: the default sign-in and
  named connections, never an account id or address.
- `ask connections` appends `(same account as …)` to a line.
- It is a notice and never refuses: two grants of one account are legitimate (a disposable grant for testing, as `claude-a`).
