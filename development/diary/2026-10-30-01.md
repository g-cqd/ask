# 2026-10-30 (01): the workspace by default, and approvals that show the command and leave nothing behind

The default write scope is the workspace, with the caches builds write: a real `swift build` works, a write to `$HOME` is refused. The
first full probe run after it caught two wording problems the change caused: the sandbox line listed every cache of every home and
pushed a resumed conversation off the screen, and every default run counted as strict. Both fixed.

Approvals show the whole command under the question, folded to one row with Ctrl+O to unfold, and leave no block once answered.

`--allow commands`, `--allow all` and `approvals.allow: ["commands"]` allow every command or every call; `--yes` stays as `--allow all`.
