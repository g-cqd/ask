# Diary — 2026-10-25 (15)

## MCP servers on the job watchdog (ADR-0102)

ADR-0099 admitted that a server which ignores its input closing is left running if ask is killed. The jobs already have an answer to
that (ADR-0079), so the work was a small seam, not a new mechanism: the transport reports its process, and ask's side lists it.

## What was built

`StdioProcessObserver`, `StdioTransport.waitUntilRunning()`, `WatchdogObserver`, and the job table given to the hub's transport
factory. A fifth probe, E.

## What went wrong on the way

- The first control I thought of, "run an older binary", does not exist: no older binary knows MCP. The honest control is narrower
  and still real: the same stubborn server outlives a parent that dies without a watchdog, which proves the probe can fail on the
  fixture. The step between that and "the observer is what fixes it" rests on a mutant (the hub passes none), not on a second binary.
  I did not build one: a build is about seven minutes here.
- A shell of the watchdog still showed in `pgrep` right after the tests; it was in its two-second grace and was gone five seconds later.
  I checked it was not a leak before moving on.
