Summary of implementation:
1. `BlockProgress.duration` is `nil` for elapsed durations <= 1s, hiding the label entirely.
2. Formatted with `Duration.UnitsFormatStyle(allowedUnits: [.hours, .minutes, .seconds], width: .abbreviated, zeroValueUnits: .hide)` in the active or requested locale. Zero units are omitted (e.g. `1 hr, 40 min, 30 sec` or `2 sec`, not `00:00:02`).
3. Added `ui.localizedDurations` (`"on"`/`"off"`, default `"on"`) in `UISection`, `ConfigurationFile+UI`, `ScreenOptions`, `RunSettings+Screen`, and `BlockStyle`. When `"off"`, formats as compact clock `H:MM:SS`, `M:SS`, or `S` seconds without leading zeroes, also hidden when <= 1s.
4. Model answers (`.assistant`) do not render tool metadata headers, so they never show an elapsed label.
5. All non-tool metadata (line count, success/failure/exit code, animated runner indicator) remains aligned on row zero with continuation lines wrapping at full width.
