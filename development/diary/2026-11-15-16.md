# 2026-11-15 (16): the model's questions, through a client's form

Roadmap 0234 step 2, elicitation. Read first (Elicitation; the schema's `ElicitationPropertySchema`, `MultiSelectPropertySchema`,
`EnumOption`, `CreateElicitationResponse`): the agent sends the request `elicitation/create` with `sessionId`, optionally
`toolCallId`, `mode: "form"`, a `message` and a flat `requestedSchema`; a property is a string (one of `oneOf` titled
options), an array of them (`items.anyOf`), a number or a boolean; the client answers `accept` with `content`, `decline` or
`cancel`; a mode is asked only of a client that advertised it (`clientCapabilities.elicitation.form`, an object); a form
never asks for a secret. This settles ADR-0154's open point: a form does have a multiple choice.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| The model's questions are one form | three questions: one choice with details, several with a required note, words only | **Holds**: `q1` a string with `oneOf`, `q2` an array with `items.anyOf` and `q2_note`, `q3` a string; `required` is `q1` and `q2_note` |
| The form's answer is the person's | accepted with content | **Holds**: choices by position in the order offered, each once; what was not asked is left out |
| Anything else declines | decline, cancel, an unknown action, nothing, an error; accepted without what is required, with a choice not offered, with the wrong shape | **Holds**: nine cases |
| A client that shows no form is not asked | four shapes of "no" | **Holds**: declined at once, no request made |
| A form nobody answers does not hold a cancelled turn | a cancel while the form is open | **Holds**: abandoned as an approval is |
| The tests can fail | five mutations of the form and its reading; then the capability check and the cancellation | **Holds**: three tests red, then two. The second run first hung: the tests awaited the prompt through a task's value, which a time limit does not end. They now wait through a mailbox, and a server that never answers fails in 64 s |
| Through the real conversation | `probe-serve.py`, part H: the model calls `question`, the client answers "Right" | **Holds**, three runs: one form about call `t11`, the model's next request holds the choice, the turn goes on. The build before this fails it: "The user declined" |

Decided here: a choice is named by its own words (`const` is the label), so an answer reads as what it says; with two choices
of the same words the first is taken. An answer that lacks what the form requires is a decline, not half an answer.

Not built: a line a command wants of the person (`askInput`: a key, a code) stays declined, since a form may not carry a
secret and no command is served yet. The `question` tool is still offered to the model when the client shows no form, and
declined when called; under `-p` it is not offered at all.
