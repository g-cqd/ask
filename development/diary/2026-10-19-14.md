# Diary — 2026-10-19 (14)

## The transcript scrolls (TerminalKit 0011, pinned at `2450544`)

The screen draws through `RenderPipeline.draw(_:with:full:)`, which makes the composer's transcript rows the
pipeline's scroll region. TerminalKit detects each shift and scrolls the terminal (its ADR-0022).

| Streamed line | 120×40 | 300×80 |
|---|---|---|
| Bytes, before (`RenderBenchmarks`) | 481 | 1,035 |
| Bytes, scroll region (`PipelineProbe`) | 101 | 102 |
| Layout and flush, before (median) | 154 µs | 213 µs |
| Layout and flush, scroll region (median) | 162 µs | 243 µs |

The bytes fall 79–90 %. The frame costs 5–14 % more CPU, spent detecting the shift; most of a streamed
frame is still the composer's layout.
