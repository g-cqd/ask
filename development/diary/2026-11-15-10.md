# 2026-11-15 (10): a served turn shows its tool calls and asks for its approvals

Roadmap 0234 step 2, the fourth step of ADR-0154's order. Read first (Tool Calls, the `.md` page): a call is `tool_call` with
`toolCallId`, `title`, `name`, `kind`, `status`, `content`, `locations`, `rawInput`; its changes are `tool_call_update`; an
approval is the request `session/request_permission` with the call and options, each an id, a name and a kind that is "a hint
for an icon", and its answer is `selected` with an option's id, or `cancelled`.

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A call is told when it begins and when it ends | a directed conversation: a read, a command, a write; then the real one through `probe-serve.py` | **Holds**: `tool_call` in progress with title, name, kind and the file's absolute path; `tool_call_update` completed or failed with what it returned |
| The client is asked what the screen would ask | an approval of each kind; the real `write` and `bash` through the probe | **Holds**: the screen's question as the title, the command as content, the screen's answers as options in the same words ("Allow `echo hi`, `cat` for the session") |
| Only what was offered can be chosen | `selected` with an id not offered, an id that does not exist, a malformed outcome, `cancelled`, an error, nothing | **Holds**: each is a refusal |
| The answer is the conversation's | the probe: a write allowed once lands in the session's folder; a second, denied, writes nothing and the model reads "did not allow" | **Holds**, three runs |
| The tests can fail | six mutations at once (any id accepted, a failed call said completed, the path left relative, a write's kind, the first call instead of the last, a refused call said to run) | **Holds** for five: seven tests red. The sixth (first instead of last) survived: every test had one call. A test with three calls begun was added; it kills it |
| On one thread | `LIBDISPATCH_COOPERATIVE_POOL_STRICT=1`, the 40 tests of `AskACPTests` | **Holds** |

Decided here:

- **The options are the screen's own** (`ApprovalOption.options(for:)`, made public for it), so an answer reaches as far through
  a client as it does on the screen. Their ids are `once`, `session`, `program`, `deny`; ACP's `kind` is only its icon hint, and
  both of ask's session-wide answers say `allow_always` there. The name says how far each reaches.
- **Which call a question is about** is not said by the conversation. A call asks before it runs, so it is the call begun last
  and not yet ended; a question with no call behind it (a command of the person's own) is shown as a call of its own.
- **No deadline on the question**: a person may leave it open overnight; the connection's own two minutes would have refused
  the call for them.
- A command's kind is `execute` and its `name` is the screen's (its shell); its text is `rawInput.command`.

Not built: an edit's diff (the conversation says an edit as text, not as old and new), a call's progress and a job's output,
`rawInput` of anything but a command, the model's questions and a line of input (declined, as before). Not handled: a client
that never answers a permission holds the turn until its input ends; `session/cancel` is what ends it, and waits for the pin.
