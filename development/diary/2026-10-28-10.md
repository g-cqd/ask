## Owner report: a stalled response printed as an NSError dump (2026-10-29)

`ask: Error Domain=NSURLErrorDomain Code=-1001 "The request timed out." UserInfo={…}` against `api.anthropic.com/v1/messages`. Two faults. URLSession's
default idle timeout (60 s without data) ended the request; a provider reading a very long prompt can be silent longer before its first event, and the
providers' own clients wait 600 s (`DEFAULT_TIMEOUT` in anthropic-sdk-python and openai-python). And a failure after the response began left the body as
the bare `URLError`: described whole, and not a `ProviderStreamError`, so the loop never saw it as retryable even when nothing had been shown. Now the
session waits 600 s (`URLSessionTransport.configuration()`), the transport turns every system error into `HTTPTransportError` with the system's sentence
(`interrupted` for a body; a timeout says how long it waited), and the provider stream wraps a broken body as `.interrupted`, retryable while nothing of
the attempt was shown, "the connection failed during the response: no data arrived for 600 seconds" otherwise. Tests through the URLProtocol stub and the
scripted transport; not reproduced against the real stall.
