CI on `0967505b` failed in Release with every test passing: `AskToolsTests` "exited with unexpected signal code 13".

| Claim | Probe | Verdict |

Fixed in `CommandCheck.run`: the pipe is marked `F_SETNOSIGPIPE`, the write is the throwing one and its failure is left to the
program's status, and the watchdog starts before the write. Three tests (`CommandCheckInputTests`), the first red as CI was: the
test process killed.

This is the third CI failure in a row from the same evening's work on the check (a clock, a held thread, a pipe). Each passed
here, on ten cores with programs that start in milliseconds. What would have caught all three before the push: the one-thread
probe (`LIBDISPATCH_COOPERATIVE_POOL_STRICT=1`) and fixtures that do the slow thing on purpose. Not yet a gate; noted.
