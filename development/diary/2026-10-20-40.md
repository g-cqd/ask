# Diary — 2026-10-20 (40)

## Registering selection, clicks and folding, on both sides

The owner asked for text selection across what is drawn (copied, tunable, kept across a scroll), click actions on components,
images and long code that fold into a placeholder, and a bounded scrolling input, said it belongs in TerminalKit with the policy in
ask's settings, and that it needs studying across ask before it is built. Then they asked for everything to be merged into `main`
and registered where it belongs in both repositories. **Nothing was built and no terminal was probed.**

Registered: ask roadmap 0051 (the interaction model and its study), ADR-0044 (the policy is configuration, nothing is hidden
without a placeholder), `development/research/terminal-agent-uis-2026-10.md` (the survey of six harnesses, which until now existed only
as a table in 0048), and pointers from roadmap 0015, 0048 and the lab README. In TerminalKit: ADR-0027 and roadmap 0016 to 0019
(its diary 2026-10-20 (1) has the source reading).

### What went wrong

- **I began the TerminalKit work on its main checkout** instead of a worktree, and undid it before writing anything.
- **Two claims about the kit were wrong in my first draft** (selection had no column conversion; a bounded input had to be
  promoted from ask). Both existed in the kit and were unused. I found it by opening the neighbouring files, before committing,
  and the entries say what is there.
- **Two sentences in the research note overstated the documentation**: that opencode's mouse setting is on by default (the docs
  show `mouse: true` in an example only) and that Pi's fullscreen mode has the mouse. Both corrected.
- **My earlier prototype work lost track of what was decided.** Roadmap 0015 had to be rewritten once nothing was painted. The
  new entries mark each interaction as the owner's example or my proposal, so this cannot happen again to the same degree.
