## Mine

- Each block's place is a key in some twenty tables, so a gone block is emptied in place (`Transcript.Item.trimmed`) rather than
  removed: no key moves.
- The memory goes to the session: `SessionTree.entries` holds every entry, and the screen's messages share its strings. The owner
  chose to land the setting as it is (2026-11-11); a leaner session is a design of its own, not started.
- `Transcript.removeAll` moved to its own file first, in a commit of its own, to make room.
