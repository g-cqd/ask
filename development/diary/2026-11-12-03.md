## Mine

- The draft stays text: a message names its pictures by the tokens it still holds, resolved when it is started, queued or sent in
  a lane, so queueing, taking back and history need nothing new. The model sees the token in the text too.
- Bounding and the model check happen once, when the picture is attached (`Conversation.preparePicture`), so the caption says what
  will be sent.
- Refactors first, in commits of their own: `Conversation`'s defaults moved to their own file.
- `AgyVersionsTests`, which is in no source of this tree, ran from a stale build artifact; not this change.
