# 2026-10-27 — Antigravity catalog parity with pi-integrations

## Claim & Investigation

Investigation prompt: Why does `pi-integrations` return more/different models than `ask` for the `agy` connection?

Inspection of `pi-integrations/src/domains/agy/cloudcode.ts` and `docs/domains/agy.md` revealed a verified backend gate:
- Cloud Code's backend (`daily-cloudcode-pa.googleapis.com`) gates content on the prefix `antigravity/cli/<version>` in `User-Agent`.
- With plain `ask/0 (Antigravity subscription connector)`, the backend only returned a reduced 20-model catalog (Recommended group only had 7 Claude & GPT-OSS models, completely omitting `gemini-4-argon-*` and expanded `gemini-3.8-flash-*`). Furthermore, streaming to `gemini-4-argon-*` returned HTTP 404 NOT_FOUND.
- With `antigravity/cli/1.4.0 ask/0 (Antigravity subscription connector)`, the backend returns the full 26-model catalog (13 Recommended models including Argon and Flash 3.8), and streaming routes to `gemini-4-argon-*` properly.
