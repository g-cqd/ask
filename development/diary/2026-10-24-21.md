## The refuted assumption

I meant to build an indicator on `scrollback`, which is "lines below the view". A unit probe first, before any code: scroll back a
transcript of 40 lines, stream three lines, and look. **Refuted that the view is stable:** `scrollback` counts from the bottom, so the
three new lines slid what the reader was looking at from lines 28–36 to 31–39 while `scrollback` stayed 3. Anyone who scrolled back
to read during a long reply had the text move under their eyes, and no record mentioned it. A constant "3 lines below" over a sliding
screen would have been wrong, so the hold and the indicator are one slice.

## What was built

- `holdingView` (`ScreenController+ViewHold`): around every conversation update, a scrolled-back reader's `scrollback` grows by the rows
  the tail gained, measured by wrapping the old last block and everything after it before and after, at the last frame's width, with the
  composer's own function. Nothing happens at the end of the conversation or before a first frame, and an update that sets the
  position itself (an approval question, a cleared conversation) is left alone.
- The status line says `12 lines below` or `1 line below` after the activity and before the job count. A click on the count, End with an
  empty draft, and PageDown (unchanged) go to the end.

## What the probes showed

- **A crash from my own test:** the test for a shrinking tail killed the test process. A change that leaves fewer blocks than the one the
  hold started from built the range `39 ..< 0`, which traps; `clearConversation` only escaped it because it also resets the position.
  Fixed with a guard, and the test now asserts the clamp at zero. Crashes count as weak evidence; this one was a real defect.
- **Mutants that crashed the test process do not count:** two of mine did (they dropped the range guard too). The runner called them
  "escaped"; I replaced them with mutants that keep the guard, and both are caught. Two other escapes were real test gaps: no test
  wrapped text at a width where rows differ (now seven-letter words, five to a row at 40 columns and six at 47), and no click landed on
  the cell just past the count.
- **Real binary** (`Scripts/probe-lines-below.py`, a gated provider that holds the second half of a reply): 8 checks. The control
  (the previous binary) slides and shows no count; the new one holds the same rows, its count grows by exactly the twenty late lines,
  End returns to the end, and a click on the count does.
