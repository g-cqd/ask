# 2026-11-12 (11): light compaction, old output and reasoning left out of the request (roadmap 0032, t33)

The owner chose light edits first (2026-11-12), kept after hearing the measured gain. Once the context reaches 60 % of the compaction
threshold, entries before compaction's kept tail (80 KB by default) are sent with a tool's output as one line saying so and without
their reasoning. Nothing is recorded: `ContextClearing.reach` folds over the context's recorded response sizes, so every later
request, and a resume, sends the same prefix, and the provider's cache breaks once a clearing. A clearing is made only when it frees
as many bytes as the trigger has tokens (about a quarter of the context), at least 64 KB, so there are few of them.
