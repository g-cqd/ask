## AemiMarkdown phase 1 (roadmap 0065): numbers

The parser exists (`~/Developer/g-cqd/AemiMarkdown`, its own diary has the full ledger and the mutations). Against this diary's swift-markdown baseline, same corpus and
machine, not interleaved: **11x throughput (110 to 113 MB/s against 10.3), 6x fewer allocations per KB (43 against 270), and a 100 KB answer streamed in
40-byte deltas with a snapshot each in 0.010 s against 4.94 s**. Every CommonMark 0.31.2 example passes (652) with the extensions off, and the 20 supported
extension examples pass with them on. The open-paragraph worst case (one 100 KB paragraph, 261 µs per delta at p50) is O(paragraph), by design, and is owed an incremental inline parse.
