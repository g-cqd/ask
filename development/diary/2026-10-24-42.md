# Diary — 2026-10-24 (42)

## Cut in the input — roadmap 0051, ADR-0088

### What changed

- With a selection in the input, Ctrl-X (the control byte, or `x` with Ctrl under the keyboard protocol) removes it, puts it on the clipboard
  whatever `ui.copyOnSelect` says, and leaves the cursor at the selection's start. Without a selection it clears the draft as before.
- The status line says `ctrl+x cut` for a selection in the input.

offering the cut for a transcript selection.
