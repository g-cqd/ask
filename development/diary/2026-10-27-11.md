# Diary — 2026-10-27 (11)

## Focused-job safety and block alignment

Continued `feature/ui-refinements` from e0660ab. Implemented the requested optional
`ui.jobAutoReturnWhenFocused` (default on) and `ui.abortTurnRequiresConfirmation`
(default off), through parsing, layering, descriptions and executable screen settings.
Confirmation means two consecutive Ctrl+C presses within two seconds. Escape remains
navigation-only. Draft clearing still precedes interruption. New turns clear old arming;
finishing a turn clears its hint. With auto-return off, a removed runner's focused output
stays visible until Escape.

Tool bodies lose the extra two-column indentation, with matching wrap widths and copy
provenance. Tool failures display `[7] ✗` instead of `✗ (7)`.
