# Diary — 2026-10-25 (08)

## The MCP client's HTTP transport (ADR-0096 addendum, roadmap 0067 phase 2)

Second slice after diary 07. Everything the transport needs already existed in ask (an injectable `HTTPTransport`, `SSEDecoder`,
`EndpointPolicy`), so it is one class and its exchange. The decisions are in the ADR's addendum; the one that mattered most was making
every POST independent: the connection's writer is serial, and a POST that waited for its reply would have held up the cancel behind a
long tool call.

## What went wrong on the way

- Nothing failed at first run, which is a reason to distrust the tests, not to trust them. The mutants showed two holes: "any response
  counts as the answer" survived (a reply holding only another request's answer was taken as complete), and "keep the first session id"
  could not be told from "keep the last". The first got a test; the second was a rule nothing needed, so the guard was removed.
- Four of the first mutants did not compile (unused variables are errors here) and proved nothing; they were rewritten.
- Two of the catches rest on a test's time limit, because the thing missing was an answer that never arrives. That is real behaviour
  but weaker evidence than an assertion, and the ADR says so.
