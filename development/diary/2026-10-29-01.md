## The freed file buffer (0088 step 3, first part)

`vmmap` on the open 12 MB session: 11.5 MiB resident in an empty large malloc region, the buffer the file was read into. It had been freed;
macOS reclaims large frees lazily, which also explains why the long session's RSS dropped after paging in some runs and not others. The file
is now read 64 KB at a time and recovered line by line: 53 to 42 MiB resident, footprint 42 to 29 MB, open time unchanged in process. The
equivalence test (60 damaged logs, 9 chunk sizes including 1 and the line limit plus or minus one) found nothing, which I had expected of the
line-limit edge.
