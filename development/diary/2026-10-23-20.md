# Diary — 2026-10-23 (20)

## A click in the input places the cursor — roadmap 0051 and TerminalKit 0019, ADR-0071

### What changed

- `FrameGeometry` carries the input's first row, row count, top display row and `InputLayout`; `PromptLayout.Frame` hands them over.
- The input is a pointer region. `placeCursor(at:)` maps the clicked cell with `InputLayout.offset(atRow:column:)`; `PromptEditor.place(cursor:)`
  moves the cursor and forgets the aim of an earlier Up or Down.
- The change merged with another session's picker-row clicks (`pickerTarget`); both regions are kept, the new one is target 3.
