# Diary — 2026-10-19 (13)

## What happened

Asked to generalise providers to OpenAI-compatible servers, design an extension system in pi's fashion, mind Ollama,
and run the best and fastest model this machine can serve, then prove ask's tooling and seam against it. Also fixed
Homebrew and moved the local work to the latest Python. Decisions: ADR-0027 (providers by configuration) and
ADR-0028 (extensions are processes, hooks are in the loop). Local model notes: `development/research/local-models-2026-10.md`.
